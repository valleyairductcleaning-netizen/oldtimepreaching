Stoics and Epicureans

Historical context · Sources linked below

Acts names some Epicurean and Stoic philosophers among Paul's interlocutors. Atkinson cautions against treating those individuals as authorized representatives of their entire schools. The brief biblical account should not be expanded into a claim that either school held one uniform position.

Acts 17:18

Sources

  • J. E. Atkinson, “St Paul’s Encounter with Athenian Stoics and Epicureans” — Akroterion 61 (2016), 43–58
