The Stoa Basileios proposal

Historical context · Sources linked below

The Stoa Basileios stood at the Agora's northwest corner and is described by the Foundation of the Hellenic World as the seat of the Archon Basileus, with religious and juridical duties. Hemer discusses the stoa as one possible council setting. Neither source establishes that Paul's speech happened there.

Acts 17:19–22

Sources

  • Foundation of the Hellenic World, “Stoa Basileios” — Building location and stated civic role
  • Colin J. Hemer, “The Speeches of Acts: II. The Areopagus Address” — Discusses the hill/council and Agora proposals
