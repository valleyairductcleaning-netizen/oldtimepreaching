The Agora as a civic center

Historical context · Sources linked below

Excavation evidence identifies the Agora as a center of civic activity, administration, commerce, and everyday life. This helps explain why Acts describes Paul reasoning in the marketplace. The archaeological record does not identify the exact spot where he spoke.
