The “unknown god” and the limits of archaeology

Historical context · Sources linked below

Acts 17:23 reports Paul's reference to an altar inscribed “To an unknown god.” Pausanias, writing in the second century, mentions altars of unknown gods near the harbor at Phalerum. That later passage is not evidence for an altar on the Areopagus or in the Agora. No surviving object has been verified as the specific altar in Acts.

Acts 17:23

Sources

  • Pausanias, Description of Greece 1.1.4 — Later testimony places altars of unknown gods near Phalerum
