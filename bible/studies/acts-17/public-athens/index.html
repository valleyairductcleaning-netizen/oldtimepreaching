Acts 17 historical study

A public city and a debated setting

Historical context · Sources linked below

Acts places Paul in the marketplace and then before the Areopagus. Areios pagos can refer to the rocky hill or to the council. Scholars have proposed both the hill and a council setting in the Agora; the exact venue is not settled, so the map distinguishes places from claims about where the speech occurred.

Acts 17:19–22
